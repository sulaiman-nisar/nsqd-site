## Brief

Scale fabrication piece for an IDEX display case — institutional defence and
aerospace context, hard-surface build, no margin for shortcut. The piece had
to stand the run of the show and continue into a permanent display after.

<Stat value="6 weeks" label="Brief to delivery" />

## Process

CAD reference modelled to the source vehicle, scaled and panelised for build.
Every plate, every rivet line, every hatch mapped before a single cut.

<BuildShot
  src="/images/work/apc-scale-model-process-cad.jpg"
  alt="CAD layout for the APC scale model — panel breakdown and reference plates"
  caption="CAD panelisation: every plate accounted for before the floor saw a file."
  aspect="16/9"
/>

Fiber laser cut the plate panels in **aluminium and brass**. CNC milled the
chassis from billet — flat-surface mill passes, manual cleanup on the seams.
Small-detail parts (turret rings, hatches, sighting hardware) ran on FDM for
form and SLA for the fine geometry.

<BuildPair
  leftSrc="/images/work/apc-scale-model-process-fiber.jpg"
  leftAlt="Plate panels on the fiber laser bed"
  rightSrc="/images/work/apc-scale-model-process-cnc.jpg"
  rightAlt="CNC milling the chassis from billet aluminium"
  caption="Fiber laser for the plates. CNC for the chassis."
/>

Panel-fit done dry first — every fastener taken in and out before paint.
Primer, base coat, weathering pass, sealer. Plinth fabricated separately to
museum spec and crated with the piece.

<Pull>Every panel taken in and out before paint. No surprises at install.</Pull>

## Outcome

Delivered to the IDEX display case on schedule. Held visual through the run
of the show and went straight into a permanent display after. A second
commission followed in the same fit-out programme.
