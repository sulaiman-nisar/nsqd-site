## Brief

WD-40 was formulated in 1953 to stop the skin of the SM-65 Atlas rocket
corroding. Water Displacement, 40th formula. GDT Group, WD-40's agent for the
Middle East, wanted that origin standing on their Big 5 Global stand: a full
Atlas replica in USAF livery, on a plinth that tells the story. Two weeks
from go-ahead to install.

<Stat value="2,500 mm" label="Nose tip to base plate" />

## Process

Nothing exists off the shelf at this size, so we modelled the Atlas from
reference photographs and sectioned it for the printers. Every part of the
rocket is FDM printed in-house on our Bambu Lab P1S farm: nose, body
sections, boosters, fins, skirt, the fuel line. Off the bed, filler, sanding
and polish to kill the layer lines, then 2K automotive paint and clear coat.

Livery is the USAF Atlas scheme. Silver body, USAF lettering and roundel,
blue raceway lines, red nose, boosters, fins and skirt, and a chrome-finished
fuel line running down the booster.

Inside, a steel frame takes the load and keeps it plumb: a 700 × 700 × 8 mm
base plate, a Ø40 mm centre mast and two 50 × 50 mm posts, all hidden in the
printed body. The 700 × 700 mm plinth carries the brand in WD-40 navy, a
matt-laminated printed vinyl with the logo, the "WHY 40?" headline and the
origin story.

Exhibition rulebooks don't agree on height. Some venues cap built elements at
2 m. So the rocket is built for two heights: a mid-body section lifts out and
2.5 m becomes 2 m, shape and detail intact.

## Outcome

Installed at GDT's Dubai office on 19 October 2026, 15 minutes on site, where
it stands until it moves to the Big 5 Global stand at Dubai World Trade
Centre, 25 to 28 November 2026. Printed, finished and framed in-house. One
unit, two weeks.
