## Brief

1Rebel ran a fitness pop-up in Dubai and needed a prize-wheel mechanic at the
door — physical, branded, mechanically reliable. Brief landed two weeks out
from the opening date.

<Stat value="14 days" label="Brief to opening day" />

## Process

CO2 laser cut the wheel body and prize segments — clean kerf, no scorch on
the cut face. UV flatbed printed the prize sectors and the 1Rebel brand-marks
direct to substrate, no decals. Bearings sourced and bench-tested for the
spin action — weighted, not floppy.

## Outcome

Drop-in, plug-and-spin. Signups captured at the wheel. Fourteen days from
brief to opening day, hit on schedule. The wheel travelled to two further
pop-ups on the same rig.
