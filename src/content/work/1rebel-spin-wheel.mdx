## Brief

1Rebel ran a fitness pop-up in Dubai and needed a prize-wheel mechanic at the
door — physical, branded, mechanically reliable. Brief landed two weeks out from
the opening date.

## Process

CO2 laser cut the wheel body and prize segments in MDF — clean kerf, no scorch
on the cut face. UV flatbed printed prize sectors and the 1Rebel brand-marks
direct to substrate, no decals. Bearings sourced and bench-tested for the spin
action — needs to feel weighted, not floppy. On-site assembly, levelled on the
plinth, and stress-tested before doors opened.

## Outcome

Drop-in, plug-and-spin. Signups captured at the wheel. Fourteen days from brief
to opening day, hit on schedule. The wheel travelled to two further pop-ups on
the same rig.
