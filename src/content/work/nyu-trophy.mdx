## Brief

Institutional sporting award for the NYU Abu Dhabi basketball programme —
should carry the weight of a trophy, not the gloss of a print.

<Stat value="Year on year" label="Reordered every season since" />

## Process

Modelled in CAD from the university's brief. SLA-printed in two sections for
resolution where it counts — net texture, plaque face. Resin finishing,
sanding, primer, metallic paint. Plaque inlay engraved separately and bonded.
Mounted on a hardwood base.

<Pull>Heavier in the hand than a print has any right to be — that's the finishing doing the work.</Pull>

## Outcome

Delivered ahead of the awards ceremony. Year-on-year reorders followed —
the trophy goes back into circulation for each new season.
