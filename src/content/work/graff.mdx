## Brief

A bamboo hand fan and velvet-pouch kit for a Graff regional store opening — a
heritage object delivered with maison-grade execution. Discreet, exact, on time.

## Process

Bamboo ribs CNC-cut from clean stock — slow feed, clean edge, no scorch on the
face. The Graff-G pattern printed in saturated green direct to fabric leaves
before assembly, register marks on every panel. Velvet pouches custom-sewn and
debossed with the maison wordmark. Whole kit hand-assembled and inspected piece
by piece.

## Outcome

Delivered ahead of the store opening. Discreet, exact, on time — and quiet
enough on the floor that the kit went into subsequent regional activations.
