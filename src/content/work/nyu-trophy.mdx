## Brief

Institutional sporting award for the NYU Abu Dhabi basketball programme —
should carry the weight of a trophy, not the gloss of a print.

## Process

Modelled in CAD from the university's brief. SLA-printed in two sections for
resolution where it counts — net texture, plaque face. Resin finishing, sanding,
primer, metallic paint. Plaque inlay engraved separately and bonded. Mounted on
a hardwood base.

## Outcome

Delivered ahead of the awards ceremony. Heavier in the hand than a print has
any right to be — that's the finishing doing the work. Year-on-year reorders
followed.
