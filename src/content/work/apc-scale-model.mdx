## Brief

Scale fabrication piece for an IDEX display case — institutional setting,
hard-surface build, no margin for shortcut.

## Process

CAD reference modelled to the source vehicle, scaled and panelised for build.
Fiber laser cut the plate panels in aluminium and brass. CNC milled the chassis
from billet. FDM and SLA printed the small-detail parts — turret rings,
hatches, sighting hardware. Panel-fit, primer, paint, and final finishing in-house.
Mounted on a custom plinth for the display case.

## Outcome

Delivered to the display case on schedule. Held visual through the run of the
show and went straight into a permanent display after. A second commission
followed in the same fit-out.
