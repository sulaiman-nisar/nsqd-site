## Brief

DoubleTree by Hilton Dubai Al Jadaf wanted a guest activation with no plug
and no screen: a bike you pedal to blend your own drink. Two of them, each
carrying a different brand. One for the hotel itself, built around the warm
cookie every DoubleTree guest gets at check-in. One for StrEat Culture, the
hotel's bar, in black and gold.

<Stat value="2" label="Bikes, two brands, one hotel" />

## Process

Visuals first. Each bike was mocked up on the base frame so the hotel could
sign off the whole treatment before anything was printed: wheel disc, brand
plate, frame lettering, the lot.

The wheel discs do the talking. A full-bleed print, laminated and fixed to a
white rim ring that screws through to the wheel, so the graphic sits flat
and stays put when the wheel is spinning under a rider. DoubleTree's disc is
the cookie, edge to edge. StrEat Culture's is a monochrome street scene with
gold bubbles rising on one side and gold drops falling on the other.

The blender housing takes a printed brand plate on each side, DoubleTree in
lime, StrEat Culture in white on black. Down the tube, cut vinyl: "Your Warm
Cookie Awaits" with cookie motifs at each end on the navy bike, "StrEat
Culture Dubai" in gold on the black one. Leather saddle and grips, chrome
horn, wicker basket. The kind of thing a guest wants to photograph before
they ride it.

## Outcome

Two bikes delivered to the hotel in September 2024, one for the lobby story
and one for the bar. Guests pedal, the jug spins, the drink is theirs. No
power needed, so the bikes go wherever the activation does.
